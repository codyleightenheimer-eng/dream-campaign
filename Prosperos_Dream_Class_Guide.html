Character Creation Reference

Class Guide

Every class available for this campaign — stat modifiers, Trauma Response, and Stress Vents for playing each one at the table.

Once per session: each listed Stress Vent can only be used once per session to actually remove Stress — the number in front of it is how many Stress points it relieves. Playing the same vent again later in the same session is fine roleplay, but it won't relieve Stress a second time that session.
Starting classes: every character starts as a Core class (Marine, Android, Scientist, Teamster) or the Suit. Every other class on this page becomes available only after a character dies, or when a player chooses to retire a character.

Marine

Core

Player's Survival Guide

✓ Starting Class

Handy in a fight, but whenever they Panic it may cause problems for the rest of the crew.

+10 Combat · +10 Body Save · +20 Fear Save · +1 Max Wounds

Trauma ResponseWhenever you Panic, every Close friendly player must make a Fear Save.

Stress Vents

  • Bark orders or discipline someone out of turn.
  • Check and recheck your weapon obsessively.
  • Pick a fight over something trivial.
  • Refuse to retreat even when it's the smart move.
  • Put yourself between danger and the crew, whether they asked for it or not.

Android

Core

Player's Survival Guide

✓ Starting Class

A terrifying and exciting addition to any crew — tends to unnerve other crewmembers with cold inhumanity.

+20 Intellect · -10 to 1 Stat · +60 Fear Save · +1 Max Wounds

Trauma ResponseFear Saves made by Close friendly players are at Disadvantage.

Stress Vents

  • Remind someone to focus on the mission.
  • Criticise someone for breaking a law, contract, or policy.
  • Do something freakish and inhuman.
  • Refuse to do something helpful because it goes against your programming.
  • Put someone else in danger through your dedication to the mission.

Scientist

Core

Player's Survival Guide

✓ Starting Class

Doctors, researchers, or anyone who wants to slice open creatures (or infected crewmembers) with a scalpel.

+10 Intellect · +5 to 1 Stat · +30 Sanity Save

Trauma ResponseWhenever you fail a Sanity Save, all Close friendly players gain 1 Stress.

Stress Vents

  • Explain something related to your expertise.
  • Document or take a sample of something horrific.
  • Cry for help.
  • Show that the horror has taught you something new.
  • Endanger yourself or others with your curiosity.

Teamster

Core

Player's Survival Guide

✓ Starting Class

Rough and tumble blue-collar space workers, mechanics, engineers, miners, and pilots.

+5 to all Stats · +10 to all Saves

Trauma ResponseOnce per session, you may take Advantage on a Panic Check.

Stress Vents

  • Complain loudly about hazard pay or working conditions.
  • Fixate on the cargo manifest or the state of the ship.
  • Cut corners to save time or money.
  • Refuse a task that isn't technically "in the contract."
  • Put the payday ahead of someone's safety.

Mobster

Standalone

Exit, Pursued by a Dream

Prospero's Dream-flavored criminal — built around faction loyalty and street-level muscle work.

Stress Vents

  • Talk about family, loyalty, or "the code."
  • Threaten someone who's crossed a line.
  • Do a favor to build leverage, not out of kindness.
  • Refuse to abandon a debt or a grudge.
  • Escalate a personal beef into everyone's problem.

Smuggler

Standalone

The Smuggler's Guide to 1365

Built for illicit cargo runs and evading Port Security — pairs with the Fence and Smuggling Contract Generators.

Stress Vents

  • Lie reflexively, even when the truth would be easier.
  • Case the room for exits and blind spots.
  • Lowball or haggle over something that doesn't need it.
  • Refuse to hand over cargo, no matter the cost.
  • Cut and run, leaving someone behind to cover your exit.

Convict

Standalone

Convict Class (Waco Matrixo)

Escaped or released, with a prison-specific Loadout and a paired Facility/Crime generator.

+20 Speed · +30 Sanity · -5 to 1 Save

Trauma ResponsePanic Checks made by nearby friendly players are at Disadvantage.

Stress Vents

  • Flinch at authority, badges, or being given orders.
  • Case an exit like you're already planning the next escape.
  • Hoard supplies "just in case."
  • Snap at anyone who treats you like you're still inside.
  • Turn on the crew the second it feels like a cell closing in.

The Suit

Standalone

The Suit (Joshua Kramer)

✓ Starting Class

Corporate flair, corporate money, corporate priorities — a walking stress source for the rest of the crew.

+20 Intellect/Speed split · -10 Combat · +2d10×100 starting credits

Trauma ResponseWhile traveling with a Suit, all other party members have +1 Minimum Stress.

Stress Vents

  • Cite a policy, clause, or corporate procedure.
  • Correct someone's terminology mid-crisis.
  • Try to monetize the situation.
  • Refuse to act without sign-off from someone who isn't there.
  • Sacrifice someone else's interests to protect the account.

Slices

Standalone

Death Pays All Debts

Edited mind-scan indentured labor — a body carrying someone else's memory, tied to the Bratva's economy.

Stress Vents

  • Question which memories are actually yours.
  • Recite something you "know" but can't place why.
  • Flinch at anything resembling an edit, a scan, or a wipe.
  • Follow an impulse that isn't quite yours to follow.
  • Let a stranger's borrowed memory make the call instead of you.

Labor Body

Standalone

Owe My Soul to the Company Store

A cloned worker's mind loaded into an industrial-grade body. The Concern's official line: not a person.

Stress Vents

  • Recite your designation instead of your name.
  • Downplay an injury like it doesn't count.
  • Follow a work order past the point it's safe.
  • Refuse comfort or help because "that's not for us."
  • Put your body between the crew and danger like it's expected of you.

Secret Agent

Standalone

Agent (T52-EXEMPT)

Another class as cover, a Secret Objective, and Codeword-triggered Covert Actions — until you're blown.

Stress Vents

  • Deflect a personal question with a half-truth.
  • Check your cover story is still holding.
  • Withhold something useful to protect your real objective.
  • Break cover in a way that isn't fully necessary.
  • Burn a source, a contact, or an ally to stay hidden.

Lawyer

Standalone

Breach of Contract

Built around Trial Phases, contract negotiation, and Arbitration — the party's answer to a lawsuit.

C:35 · Suitcase Gun 3d10 DMG · I:70 · W:4(15)

Stress Vents

  • Cite precedent, clause, or contract language.
  • Demand something get put in writing.
  • Point out who's liable.
  • Refuse to act without something in writing first.
  • Let a technicality cost someone something real.

Criminal

Rimward

Rimward Classes (Elliot Norwood)

Rim-frontier criminal enterprise operator.

+5 Strength OR +5 Speed · +10 Combat Body Save

Trauma ResponseWhen you must make a Fear Save, you may make another nearby friendly player make it instead of you.

Stress Vents

  • Case the room like a mark or a score.
  • Talk your way around a direct question.
  • Push a rule or a boundary just to see what happens.
  • Push your own Fear Save onto someone standing nearby.
  • Cut a deal that sells out someone on your own crew.

Ranger

Rimward

Rimward Classes (Elliot Norwood)

First responders sent to new worlds, distress calls, and dying colonies. Built to survive anything.

+10 Speed · +5 Intellect · +5 Body Save · +5 Fear Save

Trauma ResponseOnce per session when you Panic, you can choose to take 2 Stress instead.

Stress Vents

  • Read the room like a hostile environment.
  • Stockpile supplies past the point of reason.
  • Take charge of a survival decision nobody asked you to make.
  • Push the crew somewhere dangerous because you're sure you can handle it.
  • Leave someone behind because the group's odds are better without them.

Retiree

Rimward

Rimward Classes (Elliot Norwood)

Survived all the way to retirement, then left the retirement facility. Respect your elders.

+10 Strength/Combat, +15 Fear, -15 Body — OR +10 Intellect, skills, -15 Speed, -15 Body

Trauma ResponseWhen you Panic, you must make a Body Save or have a heart attack (Unconscious, death in 1d10 rounds if untreated).

Stress Vents

  • Reminisce at length about "back in my day."
  • Refuse help like you don't need it.
  • Complain about how things are done now.
  • Push your body past what it can safely take.
  • Have a genuine medical crisis at the worst possible moment.
