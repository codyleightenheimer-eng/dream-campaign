Reputation — Not Enough Scoundrels

Your Standing

Reputation Score is the sum of three tracks — Corpo, HLU, and ORRO. Your starting Rep depends on who owns your ship. Rep shifts ±1 for Favors, ±2 for Faction Jobs. Low Rep is dangerous; high Rep unlocks better Freight Contracts, Contraband access, and eventually rank advancement within a Faction.

Taking UA work — or getting caught doing it — gets a crew blacklisted and doxxed by every non-corporate faction in the sector.

Their real presence on this board is the UA Watchdog Radar below — not a job list, a threat tracker.

    ORRO Hierarchy

    Single crewmembers can join ORRO without the whole crew applying. All start as Applicants, working one unpaid job for a Runner patron before advancing.

    RankRole
    ApplicantRuns one unpaid job for a Runner. No pay, no complications allowed.
    RunnerPaid expedited rates to run cargo for an Arrow.
    ArrowReceives Contraband bonus pay; owes 15% dues to a Sponsor.
    SponsorCollects dues from Arrow crews; pays 1mcr/year to an Elder.
    ElderEarns 1mcr per Sponsor kept under their thumb.
    ORRO's actual currency is the Smuggler's Code: "Don't Look in the Box." Every ORRO run below transports unlabeled Contraband — the crew is never told what they're carrying, and it's illegal to ask.
