Endless Ways to Lose Money

Ephemera4Cheap

DuBose — Doptown alley pawn shop

A gaudy neon sign down an unmarked alley, welded to the bulkhead. Buys anything, no questions asked, at half its worth. Sells whatever his contractors pulled off the dead this week — weapons, gear, and a whole lot of junk nobody needed.

Won't touchCybermods. Won't say why unless you buy him a drink first.

Attica Destinations

The Exemplar — Coreworld tourism office

A clean, glossy office selling something stranger than gear: your own body, leased out by the hour to a rich stranger from the Core. Sells insurance and Coreworld-grade cyberware most Rim shops can't touch.

TITAN Interstellar Aftermarket

Halia Clarke — Repossessed ship auctions

Built on the bones of an abandoned dry dock. Every week, two repossessed vessels go up for blind auction — nobody knows what's aboard until the bidding's done. Also moves Class-0 dropships and escape pods outright, no auction needed.

The Craftsmen's Play

Dr. Theseus — Hidden slickworld skill market

Reached only by ten modified lifts hijacking the station's own transit tunnels. Inside: a slickworld built from impossible stone and gold, where Theseus and his Students refine stolen memories into installable Skillslicks.

Krosus Menagerie

Krosus'del — Doptown exotic pet dealer

A ritual site carved into the bones of a forgotten Doptown ruin. Krosus trains and sells creatures most stations would classify as existential threats. Getting there alive is the first test. Owning what you buy is the second.

The Chop Shop

The Babushka — Cybersurgeon, off the Choke

Overflowing with trash and grime, barely kept up by its hoarder proprietor. Every cybermod in the catalog, installed by a legendary, intensely private surgeon who lets her AI child Zhenya do all the talking.

The Stellar Burn / The Ecstasy

Sem & Indyl — Bar, club, and lower-level companionship

Dark, smoky, strobing lights. Sem's Bar handles drinks and rumors up top; a locked lift below leads to Indyl's exclusive domain, where companionship is bought at a 100cr door fee. Novo Droog leadership keeps a penthouse above the club.

Canyonheavy.market

Angus — Hacking collective & info brokerage

Access starts with a private booth at the Stellar Burn. Past the checkpoint: the Battlestations (a zero-g hacker floor thick with Daytona smoke) and Angus's own cramped office, where he brokers equipment, talent, and rumors nobody else can get.

d0c's Cyberclinic

doc & Essie Barrington — Neutral-ground public clinic

Nestled among the cramped apartments of Block (04,20), staffed by "the Clinicians" — Dreamers pulled from every faction, sworn to keep the clinic neutral ground. Emergency care, cyberware installs, tattoos, and quiet drug procurement, no faction card required.

Xiao! Long! Bao!

Uncle Bao — Trendy new dim sum spot

Best soup dumplings this side of the Milky Way, according to everyone who's tried them. Half price if you shout your order. A rival restaurant is quietly offering 15kcr for the secret recipe.

Prospero's Trust

Jules — Modular bank & day spa

An opulent, gold-plated bank that detaches from its port and drifts between stations. Open an account, build reputation with the CEO, treat yourself at the spa, or gamble on a Dream Crate.

The Ice Box

Highly illegal Synthetic Sleeving Facility & Slickbay

The Dream's slang term for its resleeving and Slickbay venue. Hundreds of Slickbay pods host virtual worlds for education, escape, and pleasure. A separate wing grows sleeves from scratch — vat-grown synthetic bodies your memories can be uploaded into.

Vitaly's Surplus

Vitaly Kozlenko — Ex-Tempest Co. Quartermaster

A converted cargo container in the Old Drydock District, TEMPEST CO. stencil still faintly visible underneath. Sells proper military-grade hardware — no bartering, no discounts, no questions about where it came from either.

Two-Ledger

Unaffiliated Fixer — No Fixed Location

No storefront. Dead drops and coded meetups across Doptown and the Choke, arranged through a contact chain that always starts with someone you already know. Sells the scrappy, improvised, and deeply illegal end of the market.

The Chandlery

Birdie Okonkwo — Dry Dock District

Rope, filters, scanners, comms, drones, stimpaks, and every other unglamorous tool that keeps a crew alive between the interesting parts. Birdie's been running this counter since before most captains on the Dream were born, and she'll tell you so whether you ask or not.

The Dry Dock

Loshe, Dockmaster — Ship Repair, Fuel & Travel

Hundreds of small ports stacked and craned into the station's outer ring. Loshe personally oversees every ship that comes through — repairs, refueling, docking, and passage off the Dream, all handled at one counter.
