    Pay Math

    Salary — set by the skill tier the job calls for: 500cr for Trained, 1,000cr for Expert, 2,000cr for Master.

    Hazard Pay — multiplies Salary by ×1 to ×5 depending on how dangerous the job actually is (the board rarely tells you which end you're getting until you're already in it).

    Expenses — flat costs the job covers on top: travel, materials, jump fuel if it's off-station. Doesn't count against your cut.

    Negotiating — per WOM, each leveraged factor (connections, reputation, information the client wants kept quiet) shifts the final offer roughly ±10%. Use it.
